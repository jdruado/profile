About Me

I am currently a second year Multimedia Studies student at UP Open University. I used to study Architecture in another university, but I quickly realized that it was not for me. I still wanted to be involved in the arts so I shifted to Multimedia.

I am very passionate when it comes to the arts, whatever form it may be. I've recently had experience in events and film production, which made me realize that I thrive within the chaos—I find the structure amidst the disorder.

Sample Work

Melancholic Beauty

Melancholic Beauty is a showcase of three works of art in different artforms by different artists that I deeply connect with.

I connected each work with a particular aspect of life tinged with melancholy: Life, Death, and Love.

Each connection is explained through my personal experience and interpretation of the works, and with the premise of exploring the beauty in melancholy.
